# Product Principles

1. **Low cost by default** — every recurring service must justify its value.
2. **One useful module at a time** — avoid broad incomplete features.
3. **Businesses remain distinct** — shared backend does not imply identical branding.
4. **Data ownership matters** — customer and business data remain exportable and controlled.
5. **Secure defaults** — HTTPS, secret isolation, adaptive password hashing, and least privilege.
6. **Repository as memory** — scope, decisions, tests, and operations live with the code.
7. **No silent scope creep** — roadmap changes require explicit planning.
8. **Stable before clever** — protect working workflows before refactoring.
9. **Modular monolith first** — split services only when scale or isolation proves the need.
10. **Migration paths are designed, not prematurely implemented**.
