# Product Roadmap

- Status: Approved planning baseline
- Rule: Only one Sprint is active at a time.

## Sprint 1 — Single-Business MVP

Goal: prove end-to-end operation for Mad Mallard Solutions.

Includes public site, customer intake, async messaging, feedback, customer authentication, customer dashboard, admin operations, SES, SSM, Terraform, and stable deployment.

## Sprint 2 — Multi-Tenant Foundation

Goal: support multiple businesses safely in one deployment.

Includes organizations, sites, domains, memberships, roles, tenant scoping, platform super admin, and initial data migration.

## Sprint 3 — Site Builder

Goal: allow each organization to configure a distinct public site.

Includes branding, themes, navigation, pages, services, forms, media, and domain configuration.

## Sprint 4 — CRM

Goal: manage leads and relationships beyond the inbox.

Includes contacts, companies, opportunities, tasks, notes, timelines, and quote foundations.

## Sprint 5 — Customer Portal

Goal: support ongoing service delivery.

Includes projects, tickets, files, notifications, customer history, and richer messaging.

## Sprint 6 — Creator and Commerce Tools

Goal: support Mad Mallards Adventures and creator businesses.

Includes link pages, affiliate links, content collections, digital downloads, creator analytics, and storefront foundations.

## Sprint 7 — AI Assistance

Goal: add measurable AI value without creating uncontrolled cost.

Includes drafting, summarization, knowledge search, suggested replies, and usage controls.

## Sprint 8 — Billing and Commercialization

Goal: support external paying organizations.

Includes Stripe, subscriptions, plans, quotas, invoices, licensing, and tenant onboarding.
