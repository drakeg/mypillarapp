# Personas

## P01 — Visitor

A person evaluating Mad Mallard Solutions. Needs clear services, trust, low-friction contact, and no account requirement before asking a question.

## P02 — Prospective customer

Has a specific technology problem. Needs structured intake, conversation continuity, expectations, and confidence that information was received.

## P03 — Registered customer

Needs a secure dashboard, conversation history, service requests, profile management, and later project/quote/file access.

## P04 — Business owner

Needs an overview of customer activity, work queues, business configuration, team permissions, and operational status.

## P05 — Business staff

Needs access only to the organizations and functions required for their job.

## P06 — Platform super administrator

Operates the shared platform, can administer all organizations and sites, and manages platform-wide configuration and incidents.

## Permission principle

A person may belong to multiple organizations with a different role in each. Platform administration is separate from organization membership.
